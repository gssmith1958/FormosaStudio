## 1. Overview & Purpose

**FormosaStudio (`FORM`)** is a local-first, hardware-integrated **Taiwanese Mandarin (`zh-TW` / 臺灣國語 / 正體字 / 注音符號 ㄅㄆㄇㄈ)** training workstation designed for a native English speaker using a **Linux desktop with an attached Wacom pen tablet and microphone**.

It combines:
1. **Context-Aware Taiwan MOE Linguistic & Polyphone (`破音字`) Engine (`FORM-REQ-0001`–`0002`):** Forward Maximum-Matching (`FMM`) word segmentation that accurately resolves Taiwanese polyphones (`長大 ㄓㄤˇ` vs. `很長 ㄔㄤˊ`, `還錢 ㄏㄨㄢˊ` vs. `還有 ㄏㄞˊ`, `銀行 ㄏㄤˊ` vs. `不行 ㄒㄧㄥˊ`), Taiwan Ministry of Education cross-strait pronunciations (`和 = ㄏㄢˋ`, `垃圾 = ㄌㄜˋ ㄙㄜˋ`, `星期 = ㄒㄧㄥ ㄑㄧˊ`, `研究 = ㄧㄢˊ ㄐㄧㄡˋ`), and 3rd-tone / `不` / `一` tone sandhi.
2. **Complete *Contemporary Chinese* (Vols. 1–6) & *Pimsleur* (Units 1–19) Curriculum (`FORM-REQ-0003`–`0005`):** Full TOCFL Novice 1 through Band B2/C1 progression, FSRS-lite spaced repetition, and 1-click JSON backup/restore—modularized under a strict **`<= 800` LOC per file** mechanical budget.
3. **Adaptive `i + 1` Graded Reader, Custom Text Importer & `口語/書面語` Register Bridge (`FORM-REQ-0006`–`0008`):** Live Known/Familiar/Target `i + 1` comprehensibility badges, paste-any-Taiwanese-text segmentation & cloze generation, and spoken-to-written register conversion.
4. **"Taiwan in 10 Minutes a Day" Practical English-Speaker Survival Track (`FORM-REQ-0009`–`0011`):**
   * Visual Household & Environment **Sticky Labels** + **12 "Swiss-Army-Knife" Survival Frames**.
   * Interactive **Taiwanese Paper Tick-Menu (`點菜單`) Simulator** (mountable with Wacom pen or mouse) for breakfast shops (`早餐店`), bento (`便當`), and beef noodle stalls.
   * **Taiwanese Spoken Pragmatics (`語助詞` & everyday `臺語` loanwords)** + **Functional Listening Gym** (`NT$` prices, `八折 = 20% off` discount math, `民國` calendar dates, `段/巷/弄/號` addresses, and receipts).
5. **Native Wacom Stylus (`PointerEvent`) Calligraphy & Taiwan MOE Stroke-Order Studio (`FORM-REQ-0012`–`0014`):** Live stylus pressure (`e.pressure`), `133–200 Hz` coalesced sampling (`getCoalescedEvents()`), Wacom eraser nib undo, left/right-handed toolbar toggle, Taiwan MOE vector stroke-order grading (`描紅 / 默寫 / 臨摹`), and `形聲字` radical-phonetic series builder.
6. **Offline WebAudio $F_0$ Pitch-Contour Visualizer & Pronunciation Coach (`FORM-REQ-0015`–`0016`):** 100% offline time-domain autocorrelation/YIN fundamental frequency ($F_0$) pitch tracking overlaid on target Chao 1–5 tone and sandhi curves, plus Taiwanese retroflex (`ㄓㄔㄕㄖ`) vs. flat sibilant (`ㄗㄘㄙ`) discrimination.
